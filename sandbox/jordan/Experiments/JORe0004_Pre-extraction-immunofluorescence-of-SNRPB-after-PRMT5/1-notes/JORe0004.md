# Pre-extraction immunofluorescence of SNRPB after PRMT5 inhibition

`JORe0004` · Jordan Example · 2026-08-24

## Objective

JORe0002 saw no redistribution of SNRPB after PRMT5i. Is more SNRPB left in the nucleus after soluble protein is extracted before fixation, as the fractionation result (ALXe0002) would predict?

## Experimental design

A549, 48 h DMSO vs PRMT5i (1 uM). Pre-extraction: CSK + 0.1% Triton X-100, 3 min on ice, then fix (P_Immunofluorescence, optional step). Anti-SNRPB, DAPI, confocal, same settings for both conditions. Measured: mean nuclear SNRPB intensity divided by mean DAPI intensity, 25 nuclei per condition. So far one coverslip per condition, one day.

## Results

Preliminary: one coverslip per condition (output of `3-code/JORe0004_summarise.py`):

```
DMSO: median 0.34, quartiles 0.27 to 0.42, 25 nuclei
PRMT5i: median 0.51, quartiles 0.45 to 0.60, 25 nuclei
```

Values per nucleus: `4-data_processed/JORe0004_retained-signal.csv`. The images were scored knowing which coverslip was which.

## Interpretation

Preliminary, n = 1 coverslip per condition and not scored blind. More SNRPB signal survives pre-extraction after PRMT5i, which is what extraction resistance would look like and would reconcile JORe0002 with ALXe0002.
