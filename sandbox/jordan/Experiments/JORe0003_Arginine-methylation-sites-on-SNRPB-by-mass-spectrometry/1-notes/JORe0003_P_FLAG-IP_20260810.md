# Anti-FLAG immunoprecipitation for mass spectrometry

`P_FLAG-IP` · version 2026-08-10

## Purpose

Purify FLAG-tagged protein from transfected cells with as little keratin and antibody carry-over as possible.

## Materials

- Anti-FLAG M2 magnetic beads
- Lysis buffer: 50 mM Tris pH 7.5, 150 mM NaCl, 0.5% NP-40, protease and phosphatase inhibitors
- 3x FLAG peptide
- Low-bind tubes

## Procedure

1. Lyse 4x 15 cm dishes per condition in 1 mL each; clear 16,000 x g 10 min 4C.
2. Bind to 40 uL beads 2 h 4C; wash 4x lysis buffer, 2x 50 mM ammonium bicarbonate.
3. Elute with 3x FLAG peptide or digest on bead; freeze at -80C.
