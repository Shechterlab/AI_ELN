SNRPB localization by immunofluorescence after PRMT5 inhibition

JORe0002 · Jordan Example · 2026-08-04

Objective

Is the increased chromatin fraction of SNRPB after PRMT5i (ALXe0002) visible as a change in nuclear distribution?

Experimental design

A549, 48 h DMSO vs PRMT5i (1 uM), anti-SNRPB IF, DAPI, confocal, 30 nuclei per condition, two coverslips per condition on two days. Scored: speckle count per nucleus and fraction of signal outside speckles.

Methods

Deviations from protocol

  • None

Results

No clear change in speckle number (median 18 in DMSO, 19 after PRMT5i) or in the diffuse nucleoplasmic signal fraction (mean 0.42 and 0.46); 30 nuclei per condition. No statistical test was done: the 30 nuclei come from two coverslips per condition, so they are not 30 independent samples. Speckles looked slightly larger after PRMT5i but this was not quantified.

Scores per nucleus: 4-data_processed/JORe0002_speckle-scores.csv.

Interpretation

Conflicts with the simple reading of ALXe0002: a biochemical shift to the chromatin fraction is not accompanied by a visible redistribution. Either the change is below IF resolution, or the fractionation shift reflects extraction resistance rather than localization.

Decision

Bring to lab meeting. Next: pre-extraction IF (0.1% Triton before fixation) to ask about extraction resistance directly.
