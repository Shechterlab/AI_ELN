Chromatin retention of FLAG-SNRPB R107K/R111K versus wild type

JORe0001 · Jordan Example · 2026-07-20

Objective

Does removing the PRMT5 target arginines on SNRPB (R107K/R111K) phenocopy PRMT5 inhibition (ALXe0002, Alex's notebook)?

Experimental design

A549 (ALXc0001, from Alex) transfected with FLAG-SNRPB WT (JORp0001) or R107K/R111K (JORp0002); 24 h; fractionation at 300 mM KCl; anti-FLAG blot (JORa0001), H3 as chromatin control (ALXa0003). n = 1.

Methods

Deviations from protocol

  • None

Results

FLAG signal chromatin fraction: WT 0.33, mutant 0.55. Expression of both constructs comparable in whole-cell input. The mutant band runs slightly higher than the wild-type band.

Fractions per lane: 4-data_processed/JORe0001_FLAG-chromatin-fraction.csv.

Interpretation

The methyl-site mutant is more chromatin-bound, the same direction as PRMT5 inhibition. Single replicate; the mutant also migrates slightly slower, which needs an explanation before this is trusted.

Decision

Repeat with n = 3 and a lane of untransfected cells before this goes in a figure.
