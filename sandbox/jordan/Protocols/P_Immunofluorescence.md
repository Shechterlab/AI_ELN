# Immunofluorescence on coverslips

`P_Immunofluorescence` · version 2026-07-15

## Purpose

Fix, permeabilize, and stain adherent cells on coverslips for confocal imaging.

## Materials

- 4% PFA in PBS
- 0.2% Triton X-100
- 3% BSA
- DAPI
- ProLong Glass
- Pre-extraction buffer (optional): CSK with 0.1% Triton X-100, on ice

## Procedure

1. Optional pre-extraction: CSK + 0.1% Triton X-100, 3 min on ice, before fixing. Removes soluble protein.
2. Fix 10 min RT; permeabilize 5 min; block 30 min.
3. Primary 1 h RT; secondary 45 min RT in the dark; DAPI 5 min; mount.
