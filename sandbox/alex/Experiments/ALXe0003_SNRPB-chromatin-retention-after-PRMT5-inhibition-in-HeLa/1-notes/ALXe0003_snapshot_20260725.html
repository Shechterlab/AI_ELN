SNRPB chromatin retention after PRMT5 inhibition in HeLa

ALXe0003 · Alex Example · 2026-07-14

Objective

Is the PRMT5i effect on SNRPB chromatin retention (ALXe0002) specific to A549?

Experimental design

As ALXe0002 but in HeLa. n = 2.

Methods

Deviations from protocol

  • None

Results

SNRPB chromatin fraction 0.28 and 0.30 (DMSO) vs 0.41 and 0.46 (PRMT5i). Same direction as A549, smaller magnitude. The second replicate had a weak H3 signal in the nucleoplasm lane, suggesting slight chromatin carry-over.

Interpretation

Effect reproduces in a second cell line, so it is not an A549 peculiarity. With n = 2 and one imperfect fractionation this is supporting, not conclusive.

Decision

Treat the A549 result as the primary finding; HeLa as supporting. No further HeLa replicates planned unless a reviewer asks.
