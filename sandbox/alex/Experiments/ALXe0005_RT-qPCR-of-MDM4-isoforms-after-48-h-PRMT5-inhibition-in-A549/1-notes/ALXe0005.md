# RT-qPCR of MDM4 isoforms after 48 h PRMT5 inhibition in A549

`ALXe0005` · Alex Example · 2026-09-01

## Objective

Before the RNA-seq (ALXe0004) comes back: does PRMT5 inhibition shift MDM4 from the full-length isoform to the exon-6-skipped isoform in A549, as reported for other cell types?

## Experimental design

A549, DMSO vs PRMT5i (EPZ015666, 1 uM) 48 h, three biological replicates per condition (the same RNA preparations as the RNA-seq libraries). Targets: MDM4-FL, MDM4-S (primers ALXi0001), reference ACTB. Technical triplicates. Prediction if the hypothesis holds: MDM4-FL goes down and MDM4-S goes up.

## Results

Preliminary: one plate, analysed by delta-delta-Ct against ACTB (output of `3-code/ALXe0005_ddCt.py`; fold change is 2^-(mean ddCt), relative to the DMSO samples):

```
MDM4-FL: PRMT5i / DMSO = 0.42 (per sample: 0.41, 0.45, 0.41)
MDM4-S: PRMT5i / DMSO = 4.53 (per sample: 3.44, 6.29, 4.32)
technical replicates left out by the 0.5 Ct rule: 0 of 54
```

Per-sample values: `4-data_processed/ALXe0005_ddCt.csv`. Melt curves showed one peak for every target. No-RT controls have not been run yet.

## Interpretation

Preliminary. The direction matches the prediction for both isoforms. Without the no-RT controls, a contribution of genomic DNA to the MDM4-FL signal is not ruled out. ACTB was assumed to be unaffected by PRMT5 inhibition; a second reference gene has not been checked.
