# SNRPB chromatin retention after 48 h PRMT5 inhibition in A549

`ALXe0002` · Alex Example · 2026-06-20

## Objective

Does PRMT5 inhibition increase the chromatin-bound fraction of SNRPB?

## Experimental design

A549 treated 48 h with DMSO or PRMT5 inhibitor (EPZ015666, 1 uM). Fractionation at 300 mM KCl (ALXe0001). Blots: SNRPB, U1-70K, H3, GAPDH. Three independent biological replicates on separate days. Quantification: chromatin / (chromatin + nucleoplasm) signal per replicate.

## Results

SNRPB chromatin fraction, DMSO against PRMT5i, three paired replicates (output of `3-code/ALXe0002_paired-t-test.py`):

```
DMSO: mean 0.31, SD 0.06, n = 3
PRMT5i: mean 0.56, SD 0.11, n = 3
paired difference: mean 0.25, 95% CI [0.11, 0.38]
paired t(2) = 7.8, p = 0.016
```

U1-70K shifted in the same direction but less; it was not quantified. Loading controls behaved.

![](../5-figures/ALXe0002_R_SNRPB-chromatin-fraction_20260708.png)

Quantification: `4-data_processed/ALXe0002_chromatin-fraction.csv`.

## Interpretation

Consistent with the hypothesis: loss of PRMT5 activity keeps more SNRPB on chromatin. With three replicates the interval is wide, so the size of the effect is known only roughly. Symmetric dimethyl-arginine loss was not directly confirmed in this experiment (no SDMA blot).

## Decision

Repeat in a second cell line, and ask Jordan to test whether the methyl-site mutant of SNRPB behaves the same way.
