SNRPB chromatin retention after 48 h PRMT5 inhibition in A549

ALXe0002 · Alex Example · 2026-06-20

Objective

Does PRMT5 inhibition increase the chromatin-bound fraction of SNRPB?

Experimental design

A549 treated 48 h with DMSO or PRMT5 inhibitor (EPZ015666, 1 uM). Fractionation at 300 mM KCl (ALXe0001). Blots: SNRPB, U1-70K, H3, GAPDH. Three independent biological replicates on separate days. Quantification: chromatin / (chromatin + nucleoplasm) signal per replicate.

Methods

Deviations from protocol

  • None

Results

SNRPB chromatin fraction, DMSO against PRMT5i, three paired replicates (output of 3-code/ALXe0002_paired-t-test.py):

DMSO: mean 0.31, SD 0.04, n = 3
PRMT5i: mean 0.58, SD 0.07, n = 3
paired difference: mean 0.27, 95% CI [0.20, 0.34]
paired t(2) = 17.7, p = 0.003

U1-70K shifted in the same direction but less (0.40 to 0.49). Loading controls behaved.

Quantification: 4-data_processed/ALXe0002_chromatin-fraction.csv.

Interpretation

Consistent with the hypothesis: loss of PRMT5 activity keeps more SNRPB on chromatin. Symmetric dimethyl-arginine loss was not directly confirmed in this experiment (no SDMA blot).

Decision

Repeat in a second cell line (ALXe0003) and test whether the methyl-site mutant phenocopies (JORe0001, in Jordan's notebook).
