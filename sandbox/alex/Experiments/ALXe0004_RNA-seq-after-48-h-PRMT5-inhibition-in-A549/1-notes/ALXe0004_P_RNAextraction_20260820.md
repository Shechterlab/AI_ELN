# RNA extraction (column, on-column DNase)

`P_RNAextraction` · version 2026-08-20

## Purpose

Total RNA from adherent cells for RNA-seq and RT-qPCR.

## Materials

- Lysis buffer with 2-mercaptoethanol
- Silica columns
- DNase I, on-column
- RNase-free water

## Procedure

1. Lyse one 6-well of cells in 350 uL; homogenise through a 21G needle.
2. Bind, wash, DNase 15 min on the column, wash twice, elute in 30 uL.
3. Measure concentration and A260/280; run 200 ng on a gel or TapeStation before sequencing.
