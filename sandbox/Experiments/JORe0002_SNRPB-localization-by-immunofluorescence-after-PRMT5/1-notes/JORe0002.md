# SNRPB localization by immunofluorescence after PRMT5 inhibition

`JORe0002` · Jordan Example · 2026-08-04

## Objective

Is the increased chromatin fraction of SNRPB after PRMT5i visible as a change in nuclear distribution?

## Experimental design

A549, 48 h DMSO vs PRMT5i (1 uM), anti-SNRPB IF, DAPI, confocal, 30 nuclei per condition, two coverslips per condition on two days. Scored: speckle count per nucleus and fraction of signal outside speckles.

## Methods

## Deviations from protocol

- None

## Results

No change in speckle number (median 18 vs 19) or in diffuse nucleoplasmic signal fraction (0.42 vs 0.44). Speckles looked slightly larger after PRMT5i but this was not quantified.

## Interpretation

Conflicts with the simple reading of ALXe0002: a biochemical shift to the chromatin fraction is not accompanied by a visible redistribution. Either the change is below IF resolution, or the fractionation shift reflects extraction resistance rather than localization.

## Decision

Discuss at lab meeting. Candidate next step: pre-extraction (0.1% Triton before fixation) IF to ask about extraction resistance directly.
