# Immunofluorescence on coverslips

`P_Immunofluorescence` · version 2026-07-15

## Purpose

Fix, permeabilize, and stain adherent cells on coverslips for confocal imaging.

## Materials

- 4% PFA in PBS
- 0.2% Triton X-100
- 3% BSA
- DAPI
- ProLong Glass

## Procedure

1. Fix 10 min RT; permeabilize 5 min; block 30 min.
2. Primary 1 h RT; secondary 45 min RT in the dark; DAPI 5 min; mount.
